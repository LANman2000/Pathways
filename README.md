# Pathways
A student uploads their transcript and resume, selects their major and interests, and in under 10 seconds receives personalized career pathways, skill-gap analysis, project recommendations, certifications, and internship targets.

## Account access module

[Email/password signup and login](apps/account-access/README.md) opens the interactive prototype in `HTML/`. Profile answers and PDF uploads flow into the authenticated pathway-analysis API; recommendations and saved pathways are shown in the demo.
